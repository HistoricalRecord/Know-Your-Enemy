FILE 1948 the beginning of the modern archive

The State of Israel declared independence in May 1948. Fighting involving the new state and neighboring Arab states followed.

The resulting war produced enormous population displacement and became foundational to competing Israeli and Palestinian historical narratives.

Same history different memories different archives different names.

FILE 1967 six days that changed the map

In June 1967 Israel fought Egypt Jordan and Syria in a war lasting six days.

Israel captured the Sinai Peninsula Gaza Strip West Bank East Jerusalem and Golan Heights.

The consequences of the war remain central to disputes concerning borders occupation settlements security Palestinian statehood and international law.

FILE 1973 Yom Kippur / Ramadan / October War

On October 6 1973 Egypt and Syria launched attacks against Israeli forces. The date coincided with Yom Kippur the Jewish Day of Atonement and occurred during Ramadan.

The United States supplied Israel during the conflict while the Soviet Union supported Arab states. The war eventually produced diplomatic negotiations that contributed to later disengagement agreements.

The coincidence of religious dates is historically notable. It is not by itself evidence of a hidden religious conspiracy.

RELIGION & SYMBOLS tradition / mythology / historical context

Yom Kippur is the Jewish Day of Atonement. It is associated with fasting prayer repentance and atonement.

Leviticus 16 describes an ancient ritual involving two goats. One was associated with sacrifice while another was sent into the wilderness after the community's sins were symbolically placed upon it.

The English expression scapegoat developed from this tradition.

ARCHIVE DISTINCTION: An ancient religious symbol can be historically interesting without providing evidence for a modern political claim.
GAZA FILE war / humanitarian records / international law

Gaza has generated an enormous documentary record including government statements military statements journalism humanitarian reports photographs satellite imagery court proceedings witness testimony and competing casualty estimates.

Israel states that its military campaign is directed against Hamas and is undertaken in self-defense. Palestinian officials and international organizations have made extensive allegations concerning Israeli conduct.

The International Court of Justice has issued proceedings concerning Israel and the Occupied Palestinian Territory.

Court proceedings allegations provisional measures advisory opinions and final judgments should not be treated as interchangeable.

International Court of Justice

CRIME FILES organized crime / trafficking / money

Organized crime is international by nature. Criminal organizations may operate across several countries without representing the governments or populations of those countries.

U.S. federal prosecutors have prosecuted individuals connected to organized-crime networks associated with Israel. Those cases involve allegations and criminal conduct by specific defendants.

A criminal case involving an Israeli individual or network does not establish that the Israeli government or Jewish people as a whole participated in the conduct.

U.S. Department of Justice

CONSPIRACY FILE theories / allegations / myths / documented narratives

This is the section where the archive becomes dangerous because conspiracy theories can begin with real documents real wars real intelligence operations real criminal cases real political influence or real historical discrimination and then make claims that extend far beyond the available evidence.

There is a long history of antisemitic conspiracy narratives claiming that Jews secretly control governments banks media wars or world events. Similar narratives have repeatedly appeared in different countries and historical periods.

Other conspiracy narratives specifically accuse Israel of secretly controlling or manipulating unrelated governments or international events.

THEORY FILE: These claims are archived because they are historically significant conspiracy narratives. Their presence here does not mean the archive accepts them as factual.
