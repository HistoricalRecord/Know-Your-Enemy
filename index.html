[1973 / YOM KIPPUR WAR]

October 1973 Egyptian and Syrian forces launched attacks against Israel the attack coincided with Yom Kippur the Jewish Day of Atonement and with the Islamic month of Ramadan the war became one of the defining military conflicts of the Cold War era in the Middle East

The United States subsequently conducted a major resupply effort for Israel while the Soviet Union supported Arab states and the conflict eventually contributed to intense diplomatic activity and the later disengagement agreements

QUESTION HOW MUCH OF HISTORY IS DETERMINED BY THE EVENT AND HOW MUCH BY THE NAME GIVEN TO THE EVENT

[YOM KIPPUR]

Yom Kippur means Day of Atonement it is one of the most important holy days in Judaism traditionally associated with fasting prayer repentance confession and atonement

the ancient ritual described in Leviticus 16 includes two goats one associated with sacrifice and another associated with the ritual sending away of the community's sins into the wilderness

the English word scapegoat eventually became a general expression for a person or group blamed for a problem especially when responsibility is unfairly assigned

ARCHIVE WARNING The existence of an ancient religious ritual involving symbolic transfer of wrongdoing does not establish a connection to modern political conspiracies

Israel has been a major subject of American congressional debate for decades Congress participates in foreign aid appropriations arms policy sanctions diplomatic policy and legislation concerning the Middle East

[THE WORD COLlUSION]

collusion cooperation coordination alliance influence lobbying intelligence sharing military assistance strategic partnership covert action secret agreement

these words are not interchangeable

if two governments publicly cooperate that is cooperation if officials secretly coordinate something that is another question if somebody alleges a secret agreement the allegation needs evidence if the evidence is classified the public record may not be enough to establish what happened
